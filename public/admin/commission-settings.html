💡 How Commissions Work

  • Origination Fee: One-time bonus when business first converts to Pro
  • Recurring: Monthly payments or once/year for annual subscriptions
  • Church: Tithe goes to affiliated church (or Kingdom Fund if unchurched)
  • Sales Agent: Commission to agent who signed the business
  • Sales Director: Override commission on all their agents' sales
  • Kingdom Connect: Remainder for platform operations
