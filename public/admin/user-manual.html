Chapter 2: Managing Businesses

Approval Workflow

Businesses go through a two-step approval process:

  1. Church Approval - The affiliated church must approve first
  2. Platform Approval - You approve after church approval

Reviewing a Business

  1. Go to Admin → Businesses
  2. Filter by Pending Review
  3. Click on a business to view details
  4. Review: business name, category, contact info, church affiliation
  5. Click Approve or Reject

Approval Criteria

  • Business name is appropriate and professional
  • Category matches the business type
  • Contact information is valid
  • Church affiliation is verified
  • No inappropriate content in description
Important: Rejected businesses receive an email explaining the decision. Be thoughtful in rejections.

Chapter 3: Managing Churches

Reviewing a Church

  1. Go to Admin → Churches
  2. Filter by Pending
  3. Click on a church to view details
  4. Verify: church name, denomination, address, contact
  5. Click Approve or Reject

Approval Criteria

  • Church name is legitimate
  • Denomination is appropriate for the platform
  • Address is a real church location
  • Contact information is valid
Note: Once a church is approved, it appears in the church directory and can be selected for business affiliations.

Chapter 4: Managing Members

Viewing Members

  1. Go to Admin → Members
  2. Use filters to search by name, email, or status
  3. Click on a member to view details

Member Statuses

  • Pending - Awaiting church approval (if affiliated)
  • Active - Full access to platform
  • Suspended - Temporarily disabled

Approving Members

Members affiliated with a church need church admin approval first. You can override this if needed.

Chapter 5: Categories

Managing Categories

  1. Go to Admin → Categories
  2. View existing categories and their structure
  3. Add new categories as needed
  4. Edit category names, slugs, and display order

Category Structure

  • Primary Categories - Main business types (e.g., Food & Dining, Services)
  • Subcategories - Specific types under each primary (e.g., Restaurants, Catering)
Tip: Keep categories broad enough to be useful but specific enough to help users find what they need.

Chapter 6: Email Templates

Template Categories

  • Confirmations - Submission received emails
  • Welcome - Approval welcome emails
  • Approvals - Account/listing approved
  • Rejections - Account/listing declined
  • Notifications - Activity notifications
  • Sales & Marketing - Outreach templates

Editing Templates

  1. Go to Admin → Email Templates
  2. Select a template category
  3. Choose the specific template
  4. Edit subject, headline, intro, highlights, and CTA
  5. Preview the email
  6. Save changes

Template Variables

Use these variables in your templates:

  • {{user_name}} - Member name
  • {{owner_name}} - Business owner name
  • {{business_name}} - Business name
  • {{church_name}} - Church name
  • {{recipient_name}} - Generic recipient
  • {{dashboard_url}} - Link to dashboard

Chapter 7: Presentations

Sending Presentations

Send PowerPoint presentations to prospects:

  1. Go to Admin → Presentations
  2. Select the recipient type (church, business, sales agent, member)
  3. Enter recipient email
  4. Choose whether to include PowerPoint attachment
  5. Preview and send

Presentation Types

  • Church Presentations - Benefits of listing their church
  • Business Presentations - Pro tier benefits and features
  • Sales Agent Presentations - Commission structure and opportunity
  • Member Presentations - Platform benefits

Chapter 8: Pricing & Payments

Pricing Settings

  1. Go to Admin → Pricing Settings
  2. Configure Pro tier monthly and yearly prices
  3. Set trial period duration
  4. Configure tithe percentage (default 10%)

Viewing Payments

  1. Go to Admin → Payments
  2. View all Stripe transactions
  3. Filter by date, status, or amount
  4. Click on a payment to see details
Note: Stripe handles all payment processing. You can access the full Stripe dashboard for refunds and detailed transaction data.
