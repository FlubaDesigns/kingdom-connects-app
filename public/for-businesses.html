Frequently Asked Questions

Is the free listing really free forever?

Yes! Your free listing never expires. Even if you upgrade to Pro and later downgrade, your basic listing remains active permanently. We believe in supporting Christian businesses long-term.

What happens if I cancel my Pro subscription?

Your listing automatically downgrades to the free tier. You keep your business profile, reviews, and contact information. You simply lose the Pro features like priority placement and photo galleries.

How do I know churches will actually see my listing?

Churches can feature their preferred local businesses on their profiles, and church members actively search our directory. Pro members get priority placement in search results, making you more visible to this engaged audience.

Can I upgrade from free to Pro anytime?

Absolutely! Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your business dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and you can try Pro risk-free with a 30-day trial.

What categories can I list under?

We have 40+ categories covering everything from contractors to professional services. Free listings get one primary category. Pro members can select multiple categories for broader visibility. Don't see your category? Suggest it and we'll add it!

How does the 10% giving back work?

10% of gross revenue from Pro subscriptions is allocated to churches and ministry outreach programs. This ensures Kingdom Connects actively supports the Body of Christ while helping your business grow.
