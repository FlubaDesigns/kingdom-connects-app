Chapter 1: Getting Started

Creating Your Business Listing

Before you begin:

  • Have your business information ready (address, phone, hours, etc.)
  • Know which church your business is affiliated with
  • Decide on your primary business category
  • Have your logo/photos ready (if upgrading to Pro)

Step-by-step process:

  1. Create Your Account - Go to kingdomconnects.org/signup.html, fill in your information, and click Create Account
  2. Select "Business Owner" - On the Welcome Dashboard, select the Business Owner option and click Continue
  3. Submit Your Business Listing - Fill in all required fields including your church affiliation
  4. Wait for Approval - Two-step process: Church approval first (1-3 days), then Platform admin approval (24-48 hours)
  5. Access Your Dashboard - Log in to manage your listing, respond to reviews, and upgrade to Pro

Required Information

Business Basics:

  • Business Name - Your registered business name
  • Your Name - Primary contact person
  • Email - Business email (customers will see this)
  • Phone - Business phone (customers will see this)

Address:

  • Street Address - Physical location
  • City - Must match your state
  • State - 2-letter code (e.g., FL, TX, CA)
  • ZIP Code - 5-digit postal code

Classification:

  • Primary Category - Choose ONE main category
  • Church Affiliation - Select your church (REQUIRED)
Note: Your church admin must approve your affiliation before your listing goes live. If your church isn't listed, contact support@kingdomconnects.org.

Chapter 2: Managing Your Listing

Editing Your Business Information

  1. Go to Dashboard → My Listing
  2. Click Edit Listing
  3. Update any fields you want to change
  4. Click Save Changes

Uploading Photos

Free Tier: Up to 3 photos

Pro Tier: Up to 10 photos + 3 videos

Recommended photos:

  • Business logo (high resolution)
  • Storefront or office exterior
  • Products or services in action
  • Team photos
Tip: First photo is your primary image shown in search results. Use high-quality images (1200px+ wide).

Setting Business Hours

  1. Dashboard → My Listing → Hours
  2. Set opening and closing times for each day
  3. Check "Closed" for days you don't operate
  4. Click Save

Chapter 3: Reviews and Ratings

Understanding Your Reviews

Customers can leave reviews with:

  • Star Rating - 1-5 stars
  • Written Review - Text description of their experience
  • Date - When the review was posted

Responding to Reviews (Pro Feature)

Pro subscribers can respond to customer reviews:

  1. Dashboard → Reviews
  2. Click Respond on any review
  3. Write your response (max 500 characters)
  4. Click Post Response
Best Practices:
  • Respond within 24-48 hours
  • Thank customers for positive reviews
  • Address concerns professionally in negative reviews
  • Never argue or be defensive

Flagging Inappropriate Reviews

If a review violates guidelines, you can flag it for admin review:

  1. Dashboard → Reviews
  2. Click the flag icon on the review
  3. Select reason for flagging
  4. Submit for admin review

Chapter 4: Upgrading to Pro

Pro Benefits

  • Priority Listing - Appear higher in search results
  • Distance Search Visibility - Members can find you by searching "within X miles"
  • Gold Pro Badge - Stand out with a verified badge
  • More Photos - Up to 10 photos + 3 videos
  • More Categories - 5 secondary categories (vs 1 free)
  • Review Responses - Respond to customer reviews
  • Analytics - See who's viewing your listing
  • Promotions - Create special offers
  • Products & Services - Showcase what you offer
  • Testimonials - Display customer testimonials

Pricing

  • Monthly: $9/month
  • Annual: $69.99/year (save ~35%)

How to Upgrade

  1. Dashboard → Upgrade to Pro
  2. Choose Monthly or Annual plan
  3. Enter payment information
  4. Click Subscribe
  5. Pro features activate immediately
10% Tithing: A portion of your Pro subscription supports your affiliated church and Kingdom Connects' mission to build the Christian economy.

Chapter 5: Categories and Suggestions

Choosing Your Category

Your primary category is how customers find you. Choose the ONE category that best describes your main business.

Adding Secondary Categories

Free Tier: 1 primary + 1 secondary (2 total)

Pro Tier: 1 primary + 5 secondary (6 total)

Suggesting a New Category

If your business type isn't listed:

  1. Dashboard → Suggest Category
  2. Enter your suggested category name
  3. Provide a brief description
  4. Submit for admin review
