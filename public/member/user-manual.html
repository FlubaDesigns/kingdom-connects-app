Chapter 1: Getting Started

Creating Your Account

  1. Go to Sign Up Page - Visit kingdomconnects.org/signup.html or click "Sign Up" from any page
  2. Fill in Your Information - First Name, Last Name, Email, Password (8+ characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number)
  3. Agree to Terms - Read the Terms of Service and check the agreement box
  4. Verify Your Email - Check your inbox and click the verification link
  5. Access Your Dashboard - Log in to start exploring Christian businesses and churches

Logging In

  1. Go to kingdomconnects.org/login.html
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Enter your password
  4. Click Log In
Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page, enter your email, and check your inbox for a reset link.

Chapter 2: Dashboard Features

Dashboard Overview

Your Member Admin shows:

  • Saved Favorites - Number of businesses you've bookmarked
  • Reviews Written - Number of reviews you've submitted
  • Account Status - Your membership level
  • Member Since - When you joined

Church Business Recommendations

If you're affiliated with a church, your dashboard shows Businesses at Your Church - highlighting businesses owned by fellow church members.

Why this matters: Support fellow church members, find trusted Christian businesses, and keep money in the Kingdom economy.

Quick Actions

  • My Favorites - View businesses you've saved
  • My Reviews - See and manage your reviews
  • Businesses - Browse the business directory
  • Churches - Find churches in your area
  • Settings - Update your preferences

Chapter 3: Finding Businesses

Using the Business Directory

  1. Click Businesses from your dashboard
  2. Use search options: Keyword search, Category filter, Location filter
  3. Use Distance Search to find Pro businesses within X miles of you
  4. Click any business to view details
Distance Search: This premium feature shows Pro businesses near your location. Enter your zip code or allow location access to find trusted Christian businesses in your area.

Business Listings Show

  • Business Name - The company name
  • Category - Type of business
  • Location - City and state
  • Rating - Star rating from reviews
  • Pro Badge - Gold badge for Pro subscribers

Business Detail Pages

Clicking a business shows:

  • Full description
  • Contact information
  • Business hours
  • Photos and videos
  • Customer reviews
  • Map location

Chapter 4: Saving Favorites

Adding a Business to Favorites

  1. Find a business you want to save
  2. Click the heart icon or Save button
  3. Business is added to your favorites list

Viewing Your Favorites

  1. Dashboard → My Favorites
  2. See all saved businesses
  3. Click any to view details
  4. Click heart again to remove from favorites
Tip: Use favorites to build a list of trusted Christian businesses for future reference.

Chapter 5: Writing Reviews

How to Leave a Review

  1. Go to the business page
  2. Scroll to the Reviews section
  3. Click Write a Review
  4. Select your star rating (1-5 stars)
  5. Write your review (describe your experience)
  6. Click Submit Review

Review Guidelines

  • Be honest and fair
  • Describe your actual experience
  • Be respectful and constructive
  • No personal attacks or profanity
  • No promotional content or spam

Managing Your Reviews

  1. Dashboard → My Reviews
  2. See all reviews you've written
  3. Edit or delete your reviews if needed

Chapter 6: Church Affiliation

Why Affiliate with a Church?

  • See businesses at your church
  • Get personalized recommendations
  • Support your church community
  • Connect with fellow believers

How to Affiliate

  1. Dashboard → Settings → Church
  2. Search for your church by name
  3. Click Affiliate
  4. Your dashboard updates to show church businesses

Changing Your Church

  1. Dashboard → Settings → Church
  2. Click Change Church
  3. Search for your new church
  4. Click Affiliate

Chapter 7: Account Settings

Updating Your Profile

  1. Dashboard → Settings
  2. Update your name, email, or phone
  3. Click Save Changes

Changing Your Password

  1. Dashboard → Settings → Security
  2. Enter current password
  3. Enter new password (8+ characters, 1 uppercase, 1 number)
  4. Confirm new password
  5. Click Update Password

Email Preferences

Control what emails you receive:

  • New business notifications
  • Review responses
  • Platform updates
  • Marketing emails
