Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Absolutely! Upgrade to Pro or cancel anytime. No long-term contracts.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your listing downgrades to the free baseline tier. It's never deleted - you'll still appear in searches with basic information.

Do you offer refunds?

We don't offer refunds for partial months, but you can cancel anytime to prevent future charges.

How does the tithing model work?

Kingdom Connects tithes 10% of gross revenue to support churches and Christian ministries. When you upgrade to Pro, you're helping fund Kingdom work!

Can churches list for free?

Yes! Church listings are always 100% free.
