What is Kingdom Connects?

A community-powered directory where church members and faith-aligned business owners connect, grow, and support each other. Discover trusted providers and help small businesses thrive while building God's Kingdom.

Is it free?

Yes! Listing your business is always free for church members. We offer optional Pro features ($9-12/month) for enhanced visibility, but the core directory will always be free. Plus, 10% of all revenue goes back to churches and outreach programs.
