Frequently Asked Questions

Is membership really free?

Yes! Member accounts are 100% free and always will be. You get full access to the directory, can save favorites, write reviews, and receive personalized recommendations at no cost.

Do I need to be a church member to join?

No church affiliation is required. Anyone who wants to discover and support faith-based businesses is welcome. You can optionally connect with a church for enhanced recommendations.

How do I write a review?

After creating your account, visit any business listing and click the "Write a Review" button. Share your experience to help other members find great businesses.

Can I also list my business?

Absolutely! If you own a business, you can create a separate business listing in addition to your member account. Learn about business listings.

How do I find businesses near me?

Use the directory search filters to find businesses by category, city, state, or ZIP code. Pro businesses also appear in distance-based searches for even easier local discovery.
