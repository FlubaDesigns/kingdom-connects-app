Chapter 1: Brand Overview

Our Mission

Kingdom Connects exists to strengthen the Christian economy by connecting believers with faith-based businesses and supporting churches through a revenue-sharing model.

Value Proposition

  • For Members: Find trusted Christian businesses in your community
  • For Businesses: Connect with customers who share your values at just $9/month
  • For Churches: Earn 10% of Pro subscription revenue from affiliated businesses
  • For Sales Agents: Earn 20% recurring commission on every sale

Brand Voice

  • Trustworthy: Honest, transparent, reliable
  • Community-focused: Emphasize connection and mutual support
  • Faith-centered: Ground everything in Christian values
  • Professional: Business-quality with a personal touch

Chapter 3: Target Audiences

Primary Audiences

Christian Business Owners

  • Age: 30-60
  • Active in church community
  • Value faith-based relationships
  • Looking for affordable marketing solutions
  • Want to support their church

Church Leaders & Administrators

  • Pastors, elders, church staff
  • Interested in supporting member businesses
  • Open to passive revenue opportunities
  • Community-minded

Church Members

  • Active church attendees
  • Prefer to support Christian businesses
  • Value trust and community
  • Looking for service providers

Best Business Categories

These categories convert best because they rely on trust:

  • Home Services (plumbers, electricians, HVAC)
  • Professional Services (lawyers, accountants)
  • Healthcare (dentists, chiropractors, therapists)
  • Real Estate (agents, inspectors)
  • Automotive (mechanics, body shops)

Chapter 4: Marketing Channels

Church Partnerships

The most effective channel. Partner directly with churches to:

  • Present at business owner meetings
  • Include in church bulletin/newsletter
  • Provide QR codes for Sunday bulletins
  • Announce from the pulpit

Social Media

Best platforms:

  • Facebook: Church groups, local business pages
  • Instagram: Visual testimonials, success stories
  • LinkedIn: B2B outreach, professional services

Email Marketing

  • Welcome sequences for new sign-ups
  • Success story newsletters
  • Church admin updates
  • Pro tier upgrade reminders

Referral Program

Sales agents earn 20% recurring commission. Encourage:

  • Word-of-mouth from satisfied businesses
  • Church leaders recommending to congregation
  • Member-to-member referrals

Chapter 6: Partnership Strategies

Church Partnership Approach

  1. Identify key churches in your target area
  2. Connect with church admin (not just pastor)
  3. Offer to present at business owner meetings
  4. Provide materials for bulletin announcements
  5. Follow up monthly with tithe reports

Denominational Partnerships

  • Present at denominational conferences
  • Partner with district/regional leadership
  • Create denomination-specific marketing materials
  • Offer group onboarding for multiple churches

Business Association Partnerships

  • Christian Chamber of Commerce chapters
  • Faith-based networking groups
  • Industry-specific Christian associations
  • Local business alliances
Key Principle: Partnerships work best when both sides benefit. Always lead with how Kingdom Connects helps their community, not just what you want from them.
