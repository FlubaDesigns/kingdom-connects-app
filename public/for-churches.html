Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any cost for churches to join?

No! Church profiles are 100% free, forever. There are no hidden fees, subscription costs, or charges of any kind.

How much can my church receive in tithing?

It depends on how many businesses from your church have Pro status. If you have 5 members with Pro businesses at $8.99/month each, your church receives 10% of that $44.95/month automatically. The more your members grow, the more sustainable ministry funding you receive.

Can we highlight businesses outside our congregation?

No. You can only highlight businesses from your own church members as a monthly spotlight feature. However, all Kingdom Connects businesses are already listed in the directory for everyone to find—you just can't feature non-member businesses on your church profile.

How do we receive tithing payments?

Monthly tithing payments are sent via your preferred method (bank transfer, PayPal, or check). You'll set this up in your church admin dashboard after approval.

What denominations are eligible?

All Christian denominations are welcome! Whether you're Baptist, Methodist, Non-denominational, Catholic, Pentecostal, or any other Christian tradition, you can join Kingdom Connects.
