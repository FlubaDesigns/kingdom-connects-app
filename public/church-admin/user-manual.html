Chapter 1: Church Profile Setup

Getting Church Admin Access

Before you begin:

  • You must be authorized to represent the church
  • Acceptable roles: Pastor, Elder, Church Administrator, Staff Member
  • You'll need to provide proof of authorization

Step-by-step process:

  1. Contact Platform Admin
    • Email: admin@kingdomconnects.org
    • Subject: "Church Admin Access Request - [Church Name]"
    • Include: Church name, your name and role, church phone number, your email
  2. Verification - Admin may call church office or request documentation. Typically 1-2 business days.
  3. Account Upgrade - Admin upgrades your account, you receive email notification, then log in to access your dashboard.

Creating/Editing Church Information

To create church profile:

  1. Go to: kingdomconnects.org/submit-church.html
  2. Or navigate to: Dashboard → Church Profile → Edit
  3. Fill in all required fields
  4. Click Submit for Approval
  5. Wait for admin approval (1-3 business days)

To edit existing profile:

  1. Dashboard → Church Profile
  2. Click Edit Profile
  3. Make your changes
  4. Click Save Changes
  5. Changes appear immediately (no re-approval needed)

Required Church Information

Required Fields

Basic Information:

  • Church Name - Full official name
  • City - Church location city
  • State - 2-letter code (e.g., FL, TX, CA)
  • ZIP Code - 5-digit postal code
  • Denomination - Your church's denomination/affiliation

Contact Information:

  • Contact Email - Church office email (public)
  • Contact Phone - Church office phone (public)

Service Times:

  • Sunday Service - Time(s) for Sunday worship (e.g., "9:00 AM and 11:00 AM")
  • Wednesday Service - Mid-week service time if applicable
  • Other Services - Additional services, events, Bible studies

Optional Fields (Recommended)

  • Address - Street address for Google Maps
  • Website - Church website URL
  • Description - About your church (max 500 characters)
  • Parishioner Count - Estimated active members
  • Social Media - Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, LinkedIn

Uploading Church Photos

Available to all churches (no Pro requirement):

  • Up to 10 photos, up to 3 videos
  • Maximum file size: 10MB per file
  • Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, MP4, WebM
Tip: First photo is your primary image (shows in search). Use high-quality images (1200px+ wide) that show your church's welcoming atmosphere.

Chapter 2: Page Settings

Accessing Page Settings

Location: Dashboard → Page Settings

This section lets you control what visitors see on your public church page and what features are available to your affiliated businesses.

Section Visibility

Control which sections appear on your public church page:

Content Sections:

  • Show Announcements - Display church announcements
  • Show Events - Display upcoming church events
  • Show Activities - Display regular activities and ministries

Church Details:

  • Show Denomination - Display your church's denomination
  • Show Service Times - Display worship service schedule
  • Show Welcome Message - Display your church's welcome message

Contact Information:

  • Show Address - Display church physical address
  • Show Phone Number - Display church phone
  • Show Email - Display church email
  • Show Website - Display link to church website
  • Show Social Media Links - Display Facebook, Instagram, etc.

Member & Business Features

Control additional features available to your members and affiliated businesses:

Allow QR Gear Store Access

  • Default: ON (enabled)
  • What it does: When enabled, your affiliated businesses can access the QR Gear store to offer branded merchandise and products to customers
  • Turn OFF if: Your church prefers not to promote merchandise or e-commerce features

How to change:

  1. Go to Dashboard → Page Settings
  2. Scroll to "Member & Business Features"
  3. Check or uncheck "Allow QR Gear Store Access"
  4. Click Save Settings
Why this matters: This gives your church control over commerce features. Affiliated businesses will respect your church's preferences, and you can change this setting at any time.

Preview Your Public Page

  1. Click Preview Public Page button at top of Page Settings
  2. A new tab opens showing your church's public page
  3. Review the sections and information displayed
  4. Return to Page Settings to make adjustments
  5. Click Save Settings when satisfied

Chapter 3: Managing Your Business Directory

Viewing Member Businesses

To see all affiliated businesses:

  1. Dashboard → Featured Businesses
  2. Click View All Member Businesses
  3. See complete list with: Business name, Category, Owner name, Contact info, Pro status, Featured status

Sorting and filtering:

  • Sort by: Name, Category, Date Added, Rating
  • Filter by: Pro vs Free, Featured vs Not Featured
  • Search by business name or category

Featuring Member Businesses

What is Featured Placement?

  • Highlight up to 5 member businesses
  • Featured businesses appear at top of your church's business directory
  • Gold border and "Featured" badge
  • Increased visibility to church members and visitors

How to feature a business:

  1. Dashboard → Featured Businesses
  2. Click Manage Featured Listings
  3. See list of all member businesses
  4. Click Feature next to businesses you want to highlight
  5. Click Save Changes

Approving Business Affiliations

When business owners request to affiliate with your church, you'll see a notification badge on your dashboard.

How to handle pending affiliations:

  1. Click the badge or go to Dashboard → Affiliations
  2. Review each business request
  3. Verify the business owner is a member of your church
  4. Click Approve or Reject for each request
  5. Business owner receives email notification of your decision
Why this matters: You control which businesses represent your church. Approved businesses appear in your church's business directory and your church earns 10% of Pro subscription revenue from affiliated businesses.

Chapter 4: Revenue Sharing

Understanding the 10% Tithing Model

Kingdom Connects operates on a faith-based tithing principle. When member businesses upgrade to Pro, a portion of their subscription is shared with their affiliated church.

How it works:

  • Business pays Pro subscription ($9/month or $69.99/year)
  • Kingdom Connects collects the payment
  • 10% goes to the church the business is affiliated with
  • Remaining revenue supports platform operations and mission

How Revenue is Calculated

Monthly Pro Subscription ($9/month):

  • Your church receives: 10% per business
  • 10 Pro businesses = $9/month
  • 50 Pro businesses = $45/month
  • 100 Pro businesses = $90/month

Annual Pro Subscription ($69.99/year):

  • Your church receives: ~$7/year per business
  • Paid as single annual payment

Payment Schedule

  • Calculation Period: 1st to last day of month
  • Processing: 1-5 business days after month end
  • Payment Method: Direct deposit or check (your choice)
  • Minimum Payout: $10 (rolls over if under)

Chapter 5: Analytics

Church Page Views

Track how many people are visiting your church's public page.

  • This Week: Views in the last 7 days
  • This Month: Views in the current calendar month
  • All Time: Total views since your church joined

Member Business Performance

See how your affiliated businesses are performing:

  • Total Businesses: Number of businesses affiliated with your church
  • Pro Businesses: Number with Pro subscriptions
  • Conversion Rate: Percentage of Free businesses that upgraded to Pro
  • Average Rating: Average review rating across all businesses

Tithe Tracking

  • This Month's Tithe: Revenue share earned this month
  • Total Tithe (All-Time): Total revenue share since joining
  • Pending Payout: Amount waiting to be paid out

Chapter 6: Promoting Your Church

QR Code for Bulletin/Website

Generate a QR code that links directly to your church's Kingdom Connects page.

How to get your QR code:

  1. Dashboard → Church Profile
  2. Click Download QR Code
  3. Save the image file

Where to use it:

  • Church bulletin/newsletter
  • Church website
  • Welcome packets for new members
  • Business cards
  • Posters/flyers

Encouraging Member Participation

Help grow your church's business directory:

  • Announce Kingdom Connects from the pulpit
  • Include in church newsletter
  • Feature success stories of member businesses
  • Host a "Support Local Christian Business" Sunday
  • Add Kingdom Connects link to church website
Remember: The more businesses that affiliate with your church and upgrade to Pro, the more tithe revenue your church receives. It's a win-win for supporting the Kingdom economy!
