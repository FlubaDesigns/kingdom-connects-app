Dashboard Guide

Dashboard Overview

Your main dashboard displays monthly revenue, active agent count, pro conversions, and conversion rate — all pulled live from the platform database. The top performers leaderboard and recent activity feed update automatically.

Sales Applications

Review incoming agent applications. Approve or reject applicants directly from the list, or send a personalized invite email to recruit someone specific. Approved applicants are automatically granted the sales agent role.

Go to Applications

Team Management

View all active agents, set individual monthly conversion quotas, and assign agents to sales directors. Each agent card shows their total conversions and commission earned for the current period.

Go to Team

Analytics

Track KPIs, conversion funnel performance, and commission breakdowns. Use the time period selector to compare 7, 30, or 90-day windows and identify trends in your team's performance.

Go to Analytics

Reports

Full commission ledger with export capability. Filter by agent, date range, or status. Use this for payroll reconciliation and monthly performance reviews with your team.

Go to Reports
