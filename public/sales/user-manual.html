Getting Started - Your First Week

Day 1: Set Up Your Account

  1. Get your unique referral code from Dashboard → Referrals
  2. Save it somewhere easy to copy (notes app, clipboard)
  3. Your code format: SA-YOURNAME-12345

Day 2-3: Learn the Product

Know what you're selling inside and out:

Free Tier vs Pro Tier - Know the Difference
  • Free: Basic listing, name and contact only
  • Pro ($9/mo): Photos, videos, multiple categories, analytics, featured placement, review responses, promotions

Day 4-5: Make Your First 10 Contacts

Start with people you know:

  • Business owners at your church
  • Christian friends who own businesses
  • Your own service providers (plumber, electrician, etc.)

Day 6-7: Refine Your Approach

Review what worked, what didn't. Read the scripts below.

Finding Prospects

Hot Leads (Highest Conversion)

1. Free-Tier Businesses Already on Platform

These people already signed up. They know Kingdom Connects. They just need a nudge.

Find them: Dashboard → Referrals → Free Tier Businesses

Conversion rate: 20-30%

2. Church Business Owner Meetings

Partner with a church admin. Present to 20-30 business owners at once. Walk out with 10+ sign-ups.

Find them: Contact church admins, offer to present

Conversion rate: 30-50%

Best Business Categories to Target

These categories convert best because they rely on trust and referrals:

  • Home Services: Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, roofers, contractors
  • Professional Services: Lawyers, accountants, financial advisors
  • Healthcare: Dentists, chiropractors, therapists
  • Real Estate: Agents, inspectors, property managers
Why these work: These businesses spend $500-5,000/month on marketing. $9/month is nothing to them, and they understand the value of reaching Christian customers who prefer to hire fellow believers.

Word-for-Word Scripts

Use these exact words. They work.

Opening Script (Cold Contact)

"Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] with Kingdom Connects. We're a nationwide directory that helps Christian business owners connect with church members looking for trusted services.

I noticed [your business / you're not listed yet / you're on the free tier]. Do you have 2 minutes? I'd love to show you how our Pro members are getting new customers from their local church communities."

The Pitch (After They Say Yes)

"Here's how it works: When church members need a plumber, lawyer, or contractor, they come to Kingdom Connects first because they want to support fellow Christians.

Our Pro tier is just $9 a month - less than your morning coffee. You get photos, videos, featured placement, and analytics showing exactly how many people view your listing.

Plus, here's the mission part - 10% of every Pro subscription goes directly back to churches. So you're not just marketing your business, you're investing in the Kingdom."

The Close

"Based on what you've told me, it sounds like reaching more Christian customers would help your business. Does $9/month feel reasonable for that kind of exposure?"

[Wait for response]

"Great! I can help you upgrade right now. Just use my code [YOUR CODE] at checkout and you'll be live in 5 minutes."

Church Presentation Opening

"Good morning everyone. How many of you, when you need a plumber or an electrician, wish you could find someone you could trust? Someone from your church family?

That's exactly why Kingdom Connects exists. We're building a nationwide directory of Christian-owned businesses so believers can support each other.

Today I want to show you how to get YOUR business in front of thousands of church members who are actively looking for trusted Christian providers."

Handling Objections

Every "no" is just a question you haven't answered yet.

"I can't afford $9/month right now."

Say this: "I totally understand. Let me ask you - if Kingdom Connects brought you just ONE new customer this year, what would that be worth? A $200 plumbing job? A $500 legal consultation? That's a 50x return on $108/year. Most businesses tell me they can't afford NOT to be here."

"I already have enough customers."

Say this: "That's great - you're doing something right! But here's the thing: Kingdom Connects isn't just about getting more customers. It's about building the Kingdom economy. 10% of your subscription goes to churches. And when you're fully booked, you can refer work to other Christian businesses in the directory. It's about community, not just commerce."

"I don't have time to manage another profile."

Say this: "I hear you - you're busy running a business! Here's the good news: setup takes about 10 minutes, and then it runs itself. Add your photos once, and they work for you 24/7. No posting, no maintenance, no daily check-ins. Want me to walk you through the setup right now so it's done?"

"Let me think about it."

Say this: "Of course! What specific questions can I answer that would help you decide? Is it the cost, the features, or something else?"

[Address their concern, then:]

"How about this—I'll send you a quick overview. Can I check back with you in 3 days to answer any questions?"

"I tried online directories before and they didn't work."

Say this: "I get it - Yelp and Angie's List can be hit or miss. Here's what's different about Kingdom Connects: our traffic is church members actively looking for Christian businesses. These aren't random internet strangers - they're people from your local faith community who WANT to hire believers. Have you seen how many members from [Their Church] are already here?"

Your Dashboard

Tracking Your Sales

Dashboard → Commissions shows:

  • Active Subscriptions: How many Pro businesses you've sold
  • Monthly Income: Your recurring commission this month
  • Pending: Sales made, waiting to be paid
  • Paid: Money already in your account
  • Lifetime Earnings: Total you've earned since starting

Payment Schedule

  • Commissions calculated on the last day of each month
  • Payments processed within 5 business days
  • Paid via direct deposit or PayPal
  • Minimum payout: $25 (rolls over if under)

Commission Calculation

Formula: Monthly Commission = (Active Pro Subscriptions × Monthly Price) × 20%

10 subscriptions at $9/month = $90 MRR → $18/month commission

Your Referral Code

Find it at Dashboard → Referrals → My Code

Critical: Make sure every prospect enters your code at checkout! No code = no commission. Always verify before they complete payment.

Tax Information

You're an independent contractor, so keep these in mind:

  • You'll receive Form 1099-NEC if you earn $600+ per year
  • Set aside 25-30% of earnings for self-employment tax
  • Track deductible expenses: mileage, phone, marketing materials
  • Consider quarterly estimated tax payments
